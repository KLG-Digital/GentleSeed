Strategy

Radarr and Sonarr decide on eligibility; GentleSeed then ranks by seeders and, if requested, by preferred codec.

Minimum seeders

1 keeps rare torrents. 0 also allows those with no known seeders at zero.

Allow unknown seeders

Ranked after the results with known seeders.

Preferred video codec

A preference, never a filter. H.264 and H.265 both stay eligible if Radarr/Sonarr accepts them.

Codec preference strength

Low: tie-breaker. Balanced: favors the codec without sacrificing seeders. Strong: bigger advantage, without excluding the others.

Same release team across seasons

For series: favors the team (for example T3KASHi) already used for the series, or the one found on several seasons. A preference, never a filter. Low, Balanced and High: the team's release wins unless another one has about 1.4, 3 or 10 times more seeders.

Only new media

Avoids automatically picking up the media already present at the first start of the service.

Maximum searches per cycle

Limits calls to the indexers to avoid overloading them.

Accept other languages after

Number of searches with no result before GentleSeed also accepts the releases that Radarr/Sonarr refuses only because of their language (a film with no French version, a silent film…). A search is made about every 6 hours. 0 = never: the language rules are always respected.

Indexers to ignore

GentleSeed never chooses a release from a ticked indexer, in automatic or manual searches.

Radarr / Sonarr integration

Like Prowlarr: GentleSeed registers itself in Radarr and Sonarr (a "GentleSeed" notification in Settings → Connect). They then notify it immediately of a movie or series being added, without waiting for the next check.

Important: so that GentleSeed is the only one choosing the release, untick automatic search on add in Radarr/Sonarr (and "Enable Automatic Search" in Seerr/Overseerr). Otherwise you risk two downloads.
